迭代超积的"无害性"条件

## 原文摘录

> Suppose $U$ is an ultrafilter over $I$ . When does the isomorphism
> $$
> \prod_U \Big(\prod_U \mathfrak A_i\Big) \cong \prod_U \mathfrak A_i
> $$
> occur?
> I think I saw somewhere that if $U$ is a uniform $\kappa$ -complete ultrafilter over $I$ , this is the case, but I cannot find any references.
> Partial answer:
> The easy and trivial answer: If $U$ is a principal ultrafilter, say $U=\{X\subset I: j\in X\}$ , then
> $\prod_U \mathfrak A_i \cong \mathfrak A_j$ . Hence $\prod_U \prod_U \mathfrak A_i \cong \prod_U \mathfrak A_j \cong \mathfrak A_j$ .
