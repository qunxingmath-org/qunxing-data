# Fibonacci 比值型素数

## 题面

Fibonacci 比值型素数

## 原文摘录

> Letting $F(n)$ be the $n$ 'th Fibonacci number , for what $a$ and $k\ge 1$ is $F(a^{k+1})/F(a^k)$ prime?
> I know of just $6$ examples:
> $$\eqalign{
> 3 &= F(2^2)/F(2^1)\cr
> 7 &= F(2^3)/F(2^2)\cr
> 17 &= F(3^2)/F(3^1)\cr
> 47 &= F(2^4)/F(2^3)\cr
> 2207 &= F(2^5)/F(2^4)\cr
> 97415813466381445596089 &= F(11^2)/F(11^1)\cr}$$
> If it exists, any other example is greater than $10^{20000}$ .
> It's easy to show that $a$ must be prime.
