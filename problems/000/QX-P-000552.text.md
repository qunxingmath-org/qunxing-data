# 17.45 带 Sylow 塔的 Hall 子群是否强正规

## 题面

H 强正规（strongly pronormal）：对每 L≤H 与 g∈G，L^g 在 ⟨H,L^g⟩ 中共轭于 H 的子群。有限群中带 Sylow 塔的 Hall 子群是否必强正规？

### 形式化表述

见题面。

## 原文摘录

> 2010 辑问题 17.45，提出人：E. P. Vdovin, D. O. Revin〔batch_K二波〕
>
> c) A subgroup H of a group G is called pronormal if H and Hg are conjugate
> in ⟨H, Hg⟩for every g ∈G. We say that H is strongly pronormal if Lg is conjugate
> to a subgroup of H in ⟨H, Lg⟩for every L ⩽H and g ∈G.
> In a finite group, is a Hall subgroup with a Sylow tower always strongly pronormal?
> Notice that there exist finite (non-simple) groups with a non-pronormal Hall sub-
> group. Hall subgroups with a Sylow tower are known to be pronormal.
> E. P. Vdovin, D. O. Revin
>
> —— Kourovka Notebook, Unsolved Problems in Group Theory, No. 21 (arXiv:1401.0300v50, 2026-11-02)
