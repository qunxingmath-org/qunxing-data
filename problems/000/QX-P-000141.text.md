# Kourovka 19.66：Tarski 型变元簇：任意两个非交换 Θ-自由有限秩群初等等价

## 题面

Tarski 型变元簇：任意两个非交换 Θ-自由有限秩群初等等价。(a) 找全群变元簇外的例子 (b) Burnside 变元簇是 Tarski 型吗？

### 形式化表述

∃Tarski type variety ≠ all groups ? Burnside variety Tarski type ?

## 原文摘录

> 提出人：B. I. Plotkin, E. B. Plotkin
> We say that a variety Θ is of Tarski type if any two non-abelian Θ-free groups
> of finite rank are elementarily equivalent.
> a) Find examples of Tarski type varieties distinct from the variety of all groups.
> b) Is it true that the Burnside variety Bn of all groups of exponent n, where n is
> big enough, is of Tarski type?
> c) Is it true that the n-Engel variety En of all groups satisfying the identity
> [[[x, y], y], . . . , y
> |
> {z
> }
> n
> ] ≡1, where n is big enough, is of Tarski type?
