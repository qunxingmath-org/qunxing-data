# Kourovka 13.2：存在有限基变元簇 V 使 Fn(V) 字问题可解（∀n）而 F∞(V) 不可解吗

## 题面

存在有限基变元簇 V 使 Fn(V) 字问题可解（∀n）而 F∞(V) 不可解吗？

### 形式化表述

∃finitely based V: WP decidable in all Fn(V), undecidable in F∞(V) ?

## 原文摘录

> 提出人：M. I. Anokhin
> Does there exist a finitely based variety of groups V such that the word problem
> is solvable in Fn(V) for every positive integer n, but is unsolvable in F∞(V) (with
> respect to a free generating system)?
> M. I. Anokhin
>
> —— Kourovka Notebook, Unsolved Problems in Group Theory, No. 21（E. I. Khukhro & V. D. Mazurov 编），问题 13.2（第13辑 1995 年）
