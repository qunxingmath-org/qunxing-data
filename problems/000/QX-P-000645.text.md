## 原文摘录

> 2014 辑问题 18.20，提出人：V. A. Belonogov〔batch_K二波〕
>
> Characters φ and ψ of a finite group G are said to be semiproportional if
> they are not proportional and there is a normal subset M of G such that φ|M is
> proportional to ψ|M and φ|G\M is proportional to ψ|G\M.
> Conjecture: If φ and ψ are semiproportional irreducible characters of a finite
> group, then φ(1) = ψ(1).
> V. A. Belonogov
>
> —— Kourovka Notebook, Unsolved Problems in Group Theory, No. 21 (arXiv:1401.0300v50, 2026-11-02)
