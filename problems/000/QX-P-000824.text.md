# The covering radius of RM(1,9) is precisely 242

## 题面

确定更高维 Reed–Muller 码 RM(1,m)（m≥10，首推 m=10）的覆盖半径 ρ(1,m) 精确值。

### 形式化表述

ρ(1,m) = max_{v∈F_2^{2^m}} min_{c∈RM(1,m)} d(v,c)。已知：ρ(1,9)=242（原文）；m≥10 未定。

## 原文摘录

> "It is known that 242 ≤ ρ(1,9) ≤ 244, and the determination of ρ(1,9) is a long-standing open question. We give a computer-assisted proof that ρ(1,9) = 242, leveraging the classification of the 349 orbits in RM(3,9)/RM(2,9) under GL(9,2)."
> （出处: Hou, Sabo, Slaughter, arXiv:2611.00070, 2026-10-15）
>
> —— arXiv 预印本
