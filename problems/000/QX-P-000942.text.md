## 题面

对任意 t 存在 n(t)：凡有至少 n(t) 个共轭类、Fitting 高≤t 的可解极大子群者，Fitting 高≤t？

### 形式化表述

∃n(t): #(conj classes of soluble max subgroups of height ≤t) ≥ n(t) ⇒ fh(G)≤t ?

## 原文摘录

> 提出人：A. F. Vasil’ev, T. I. Vasil’eva
> Is it true that for any positive integer t there is a positive integer n(t) such
> that any finite group with at least n(t) conjugate classes of soluble maximal subgroups
> of Fitting height at most t is itself a soluble group of Fitting height at most t?
> A. F. Vasil’ev, T. I. Vasil’eva
>
> —— Kourovka Notebook, Unsolved Problems in Group Theory, No. 21（E. I. Khukhro & V. D. Mazurov 编），问题 19.99（第19辑 2018 年）
