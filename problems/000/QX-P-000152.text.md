### 形式化表述

## 原文摘录

> 提出人：D. Segal
> A pro-p group is (relatively) strictly finitely presented if it is the pro-p
> completion of a group that is finitely presented (respectively, finitely presented in
> some finitely-based variety of groups). A pro-p group is finitely axiomatizable if it
> is determined up to isomorphism by a single sentence in the first-order language of
> group theory.
> (a) Does there exist a (relatively) strictly finitely presented pro-p group that is not
> finitely axiomatizable in the class of all pro-p groups?
> (b) In particular, is every finitely generated free pro-p group finitely axiomatizable?
