# 20.82 谱可识别群的 Socle 商是否可解

## 题面

（Revin）G 有限群且每个同谱群都同构于 G（谱可识别）。G/Soc(G) 是否可解？

### 形式化表述

谱可识别 ⇒ G/Soc(G) 可解？

## 原文摘录

> Kourovka Notebook 第 2022 辑问题 20.82（第21版 (2026) 正文仍在列），提出人：D. O. Revin
>
> Two groups are said to be isospectral if they have the same set of element
> orders. Suppose that G is a finite group such that every finite group isospectral to G
> is isomorphic to G. Is it true that the quotient of G by its socle is solvable?
> D. O. Revin
>
> —— Kourovka Notebook, Unsolved Problems in Group Theory, No. 21 (arXiv:1401.0300v50, 2026-11-02, E. I. Khukhro & V. D. Mazurov 编)
