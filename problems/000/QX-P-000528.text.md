# 19.42 Cannon–Thurston 映射的纤维有限性与一致有界性

## 题面

（Kapovich）H 双曲子群≤双曲群 G 且 CT 映射 j:∂H→∂G 存在。a) 每个 p∈∂G 的完全原像 j⁻¹(p) 有限？b) 一致有界 |j⁻¹(p)|≤N(G,H)？

### 形式化表述

见题面 a/b。

## 原文摘录

> Kourovka Notebook 第 2018 辑问题 19.42（第21版 (2026) 正文仍在列），提出人：I. Kapovich
>
> Suppose that H is a word-hyperbolic subgroup of a word-hyperbolic group
> G such that the inclusion of H to G extends to a continuous H-equivariant map
> j : ∂H →∂G between their hyperbolic boundaries. If such an extension exists, it is
> unique and j is called the Cannon–Thurston map.
> a) Is it true that for every point p ∈∂G its full preimage j−1(p) is finite?
> b) Moreover, is it true that there is a number N = N(G, H) < ∞such that for
> every p ∈∂G the full preimage j−1(p) consists of at most N points?
> The answer to both questions…
>
> —— Kourovka Notebook, Unsolved Problems in Group Theory, No. 21 (arXiv:1401.0300v50, 2026-11-02, E. I. Khukhro & V. D. Mazurov 编)（节选）

## 已知结果与进展

已知存在的经典情形（surface fiber 等）纤维有限；Perry 2025 相对双曲 free-by-cyclic 情形给出非一致有界例；Hamenstädt ~2026 bounded-to-one 工作。一般 a/b 开放。

## 边界与约定

- 边界：CT映射存在性为前提；N(G,H)依赖子群对。
- 独立验证建议：候选一致有界反例沿Perry构造扩展。
