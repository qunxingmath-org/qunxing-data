# 17.60 CTP(Z) 两两不同构？

## 题面

对奇素数集 P，CTP(Z) 为仅用 P∪{2} 模数的类换位子生成的子群（各为单群）。它们两两不同构吗？

### 形式化表述

P≠Q ⇒ CTP(P)≄CTP(Q)？

## 原文摘录

> 2010 辑问题 17.60，提出人：S. Kohl〔batch_K二波〕
>
> Given a set P of odd primes, let CTP(Z) denote the subgroup of CT(Z)
> which is generated by all class transpositions which interchange residue classes whose
> moduli have only prime factors in P ∪{2}. The groups CTP(Z) are simple (Math.
> Z., 264, no. 4 (2010), 927–938). Are they pairwise nonisomorphic?
> S. Kohl
>
> —— Kourovka Notebook, Unsolved Problems in Group Theory, No. 21 (arXiv:1401.0300v50, 2026-11-02)
