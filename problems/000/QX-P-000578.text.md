# 14.57 刻画有限宽度的遗传恰无限 pro-p 群

## 题面

描述有限宽度 p^d 的遗传恰无限（hereditarily just infinite）pro-p 群。

### 形式化表述

分类任务。

## 原文摘录

> 1999 辑问题 14.57，提出人：C. R. Leedham-Green〔batch_K二波〕
>
> Describe the hereditarily just infinite pro-p-groups of finite width.
> Definitions: A pro-p-group G has finite width pd if |γi(G)/γi+1(G)| ⩽pd for all i,
> and G is hereditarily just infinite if G is infinite and each of its open subgroups has
> no closed normal subgroups of infinite index.
> C. R. Leedham-Green
>
> —— Kourovka Notebook, Unsolved Problems in Group Theory, No. 21 (arXiv:1401.0300v50, 2026-11-02)
