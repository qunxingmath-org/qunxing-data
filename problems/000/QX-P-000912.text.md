# On the representation number of chessboard graphs

## 题面

确定 bishop 图与 knight 图（及 rook、king 补全情形）的表示数 representation number：每顶点在表示词中出现次数的最小k。

### 形式化表述

representation number = min k 使图可由每字母恰出现k次的词表示（对换判据）。已知：queen 完全分类（原文）；bishop/knight 部分。

## 原文摘录

> "We provide a complete classification for queen graphs and partial classifications or observations for the other classes. As a consequence of our study, we obtain a characterization of all chessboard graphs that are circle graphs. Our work also leads to several interesting open problems."
> （出处: Kitaev, Pyatkin, arXiv:2609.22311, 2026-09-15）
>
> —— arXiv 预印本
