# Kourovka 21.13：Zω 有对偶为秩 2^{ℵ0} 自由交换群的子群（已知）

## 题面

Zω 有对偶为秩 2^{ℵ0} 自由交换群的子群（已知）。有对偶为更大秩（最大可能 2^{2^{ℵ0}}）自由交换群的子群吗？

### 形式化表述

∃H≤Z^ω: Hom(H,Z) ≅ Z^{2^{2^{ℵ0}}} free abelian ?

## 原文摘录

> 提出人：G. M. Bergman
> It is known that the group Zω has a subgroup whose dual is free abelian
> of rank 2ℵ0 (see 17.24 in Archive).
> Does Zω have a subgroup whose dual is free
> abelian of still larger rank (the largest possible being 22ℵ0)? This is Question 11 in
> (G. M. Bergman, Portugaliae Math., 69 (2012) 69–84).
> G. M. Bergman
>
> —— Kourovka Notebook, Unsolved Problems in Group Theory, No. 21（E. I. Khukhro & V. D. Mazurov 编），问题 21.13（第21辑 2026 年）
