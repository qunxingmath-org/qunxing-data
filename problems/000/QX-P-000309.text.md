# Seymour 自子式（self-minor）猜想

## 题面

除已知小例外清单外，每个有限图都同构于自身的真子式。

### 形式化表述

∀G∉例外: ∃真子集收缩型嵌入 G ≅ G' ≺ G。

## 原文摘录

> Seymour's self-minor conjecture (1980s, EGRES egres-0461): every finite graph is isomorphic to a proper minor of itself, apart from a known small list of exceptions.
>
> —— 提出: P. Seymour (1980s 提问)
来源: EGRES egres-0461
