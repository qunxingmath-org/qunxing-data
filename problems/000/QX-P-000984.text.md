# Kourovka 21.98：w 多线性换位子词，G 中 w-值集被有限多个循环子群覆盖，则言语子群 w(G) 有限-by-循环吗

## 题面

w 多线性换位子词，G 中 w-值集被有限多个循环子群覆盖，则言语子群 w(G) 有限-by-循环吗？（下中心词已真）

### 形式化表述

w-values ⊆ finite union of cyclics ⇒ w(G) finite-by-cyclic ?

## 原文摘录

> 提出人：M. Morigi
> Let w be a multilinear commutator word, and assume that G is a group where
> the set of w-values is covered by finitely many cyclic subgroups. Is it true that the
> verbal subgroup w(G) is finite-by-cyclic?
> This is true for lower central words (G. Cutolo, C. Nicotera, J. Algebra, 324, no. 7
> (2010), 1616–1624).
> M. Morigi
>
> —— Kourovka Notebook, Unsolved Problems in Group Theory, No. 21（E. I. Khukhro & V. D. Mazurov 编），问题 21.98（第21辑 2026 年）
