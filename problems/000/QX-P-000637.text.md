# 17.125 共轭对 a,b 使 π(G)=π(⟨a,b⟩)

## 题面

每个有限群是否含一对共轭元 a,b 使 π(G)=π(⟨a,b⟩)？可解群已证；已知存在 2 生成子群 H 使 π(H)=π(G)（Lucchini–Morigi–Shumyatsky 2012）。

### 形式化表述

见题面。

## 原文摘录

> 2010 辑问题 17.125，提出人：P. Shumyatsky〔batch_K二波〕
>
> Does every finite group G contain a pair of conjugate elements a, b such that
> π(G) = π(⟨a, b⟩)? This is true for soluble groups.
> Comment of 2013: it was proved in (A. Lucchini, M. Morigi, P. Shumyatsky, Forum
> Math., 24 (2012), 875–887) that every finite group G contains a 2-generator subgroup
> H such that π(G) = π(H).
> P. Shumyatsky
>
> —— Kourovka Notebook, Unsolved Problems in Group Theory, No. 21 (arXiv:1401.0300v50, 2026-11-02)
