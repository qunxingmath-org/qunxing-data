# 18.51 fp(n) 增长率：超多项式但亚指数？

## 题面

fp(n) 为使每个 pⁿ 阶群都能嵌入的最小 p^{fp(n)} 阶群之指数。fp(n) 是否增长快于多项式、慢于指数？

见题面。

## 原文摘录

> 2014 辑问题 18.51，提出人：S. Kohl〔batch_K二波〕
>
> Given a prime p and n ∈N, let fp(n) be the smallest number such that there
> is a group of order pfp(n) into which every group of order pn embeds. Is it true that
> fp(n) grows faster than polynomially but slower than exponentially when n tends to
> infinity?
> S. Kohl
>
> —— Kourovka Notebook, Unsolved Problems in Group Theory, No. 21 (arXiv:1401.0300v50, 2026-11-02)
