# 18.77 Passman 最大次数表示核交平凡（p>e）

## 题面

> Let G be a finite p-group and let pe be the largest degree of an irreducible
> complex representation of G. If p > e, is it necessarily true that T ker Θ = 1, where
> the intersection runs over all irreducible complex representations Θ of G of degree pe?
