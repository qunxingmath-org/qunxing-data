# 18.114 5′-子群 G≤GL(V)：k(V⋊G)=|V| 但 G 非循环？

## 题面

是否存在 5′-子群 G≤GL(V)（V 为有限 F₅-空间）使半直积 V⋊G 共轭类数=|V| 而 G 非循环？p=2,3 存在、p>5 不存在已知。

### 形式化表述

见题面。

## 原文摘录

> 2014 辑问题 18.114，提出人：P. Schmid〔batch_K二波〕
>
> Does there exist an irreducible 5′-subgroup G of GL(V ) for some finite F5-
> space V such that the number of conjugacy classes of the semidirect product V G is
> equal to |V | but G is not cyclic?
> Such a subgroup exists if 5 is replaced by p = 2, 3 but does not exist for primes
> p > 5 (J. Group Theory, 14 (2011), 175–199).
> P. Schmid
>
> —— Kourovka Notebook, Unsolved Problems in Group Theory, No. 21 (arXiv:1401.0300v50, 2026-11-02)
