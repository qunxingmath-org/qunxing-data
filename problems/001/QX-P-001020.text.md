# Kourovka 14.99：超根形成（Sn-封闭且含所有 F-次正规 F-子群积 G=AB）

## 题面

超根形成（Sn-封闭且含所有 F-次正规 F-子群积 G=AB）。找出所有超根局部形成

### 形式化表述

find all superradical local formations

## 原文摘录

> 提出人：L. A. Shemetkov
> a) A formation F of finite groups is called superradical if it is Sn-closed and
> contains every finite group of the form G = AB where A and B are F-subnormal
> F-subgroups. Find all superradical local formations.
> L. A. Shemetkov
>
> —— Kourovka Notebook, Unsolved Problems in Group Theory, No. 21（E. I. Khukhro & V. D. Mazurov 编），问题 14.99（第14辑 1999 年）
