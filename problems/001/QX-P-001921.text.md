# 向量丛张量逆

## 题面

向量丛的张量逆存在性

## 原文摘录

> For any vector bundle $E$ over a finite-dimensional CW complex, there is a vector bundle $E'$ such that $E\oplus E'$ is trivial.
> For a compact Hausdorff base, this is Proposition 1.4 of Hatcher's Vector Bundles and K Theory , while the general case can be deduced from Tom Goodwillie's comments here . Finite-dimensionality of the base is a necessary hypothesis as the tautological line bundle $\gamma^1 \to \mathbb{RP}^{\infty}$ demonstrates (see Example 3.6 of Hatcher).
