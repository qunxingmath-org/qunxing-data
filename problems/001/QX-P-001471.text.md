## 题面

是否存在光滑射影复簇 X：对角线有上同调分解但无 Chow 理论分解？（Q 系数下由 Bloch 猜想两者等价）

### 形式化表述

## 原文摘录

> Let $X$ be a smooth projective complex variety. If $X$ admits a Chow decomposition of the diagonal then it admits a cohomological decomposition of the diagonal. Indeed one can take the cohomological classes in the Chow decomposition to get a cohomological decomposition. Further, assuming the Bloch's conjecture, when working with $\mathbb{Q}$ -coefficients the two notions are equivalent.
