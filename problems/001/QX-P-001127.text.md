## 原文摘录

> 〔13.25 〕提出人：E. G. Zelenyuk
> Let (G, τ) be a topological group with finite semigroup τ(G) of ultrafilters converging to the identity (I. V. Protasov, Siberian Math. J., 34, no. 5 (1993), 938– 951). Is it true that τ(G) is a semigroup of idempotents? Comment of 2005: This is proved for G countable (E. G. Zelenyuk, Mat. Studii, 14 (2000), 121–140). E. G. Zelenyuk
>
> —— Kourovka Notebook, Unsolved Problems in Group Theory, No. 21（E. I. Khukhro & V. D. Mazurov 编），问题 13.25（第13辑 1995 年）
