# batch_I I-049：w⁵+x⁵+y⁵+z⁵=1（整数）是否存在非平凡解（|wxyz|>1）？

## 题面

w⁵+x⁵+y⁵+z⁵=1（整数）是否存在非平凡解（|wxyz|>1）？

### 形式化表述

∃整数 w,x,y,z: w⁵+x⁵+y⁵+z⁵=1 ∧ |wxyz|>1？

## 原文摘录

> Let $w^5+x^5+y^5+z^5=1$ such that $w,x,y,z$ are all Integers. Are there any non trivial Solutions ? EDIT: Nontrivial means $|wxyz| > 1$ . Computational Search : According to Jeffrey M. Braun Paper ,The fourth known primitive solution to $a^5+b^5+c^5+d^5=e^5$ , we can conclude that $max(|w|,|x|,|y|,|z|)<1.45×10^6$ has no non trivial solutions (search is exhaustive) and $1.45×10^6<|w|,|x|,|y|,|z|<2×10^6$ no non trivial solutions (partial search).
