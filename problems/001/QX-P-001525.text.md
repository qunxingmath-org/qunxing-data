# 19.15 L(qA) 是否含非幂零群

## 题面

对有限幂零群 A，令 L(qA) 为"每元素正规闭包属于 qA"的群类。L(qA) 会含非幂零群吗？

### 形式化表述

∃幂零A: L(qA) 含非幂零群？

## 原文摘录

> Kourovka Notebook 第 2018 辑问题 19.15（第21版 (2026) 正文仍在列），提出人：A. I. Budkin
>
> For a class of groups M, let L(M) denote the class of all groups G in which
> the normal closure ⟨aG⟩of any element a ∈G is contained in M. Let qA be the
> quasivariety generated by a finite nilpotent group A. Can the quasivariety L(qA)
> contain a non-nilpotent group?
