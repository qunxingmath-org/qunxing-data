# Can a local knot in Y bound a surface of smaller genus in Y×I than in S³×I?

## 题面

局部纽结（含于Y的3-ball中的纽结）：能否在Y×I中界出比B³×I中更小亏格的曲面（即局部性是否在有理割补中保持）？

### 形式化表述

## 原文摘录

> An open question asks whether a local knot in a 3-manifold Y, that is, a knot contained in a 3-ball, can bound a surface of smaller genus in Y×I than in S³×I.（Park-Wu-Yang, arXiv:2603.18619 摘要）
