# batch_I I-076：t∈Q[t] 是否为 Q[t] 中三个多项式的立方和？

## 题面

t∈Q[t] 是否为 Q[t] 中三个多项式的立方和？

### 形式化表述

∃f,g,h∈Q[t]: f³+g³+h³=t？

## 原文摘录

> An old problem asks whether or not the polynomial $$ t \in \mathbb{Q}[t] $$ is a sum of three cubes, (of polynomials in $\mathbb{Q}[t]$). Question: Something new known now ? Somebody has an idea of what to try (besides searching the literature available)? It is well known and easy to write $t$ as a sum of four cubes. Also, certainly, $t$ cannot be a sum of two cubes. 
>

## 已知结果与进展

已知：t 为 4 个立方之和（恒等式）；t 非 Z[t] 中三立方和（Watson：求导得 1≡0 mod 3 矛盾）；t 为 Q(t) 中三立方和（显式有理函数恒等式）；数域中三立方表示存在（Gerry：6t=(t+1)³−2t³+(t−1)³ 变形）；Vaserstein 奠基文献；Q[t] 情形无进展。
