# Kourovka 16.110：判定∀φ 循环长 φ(u)=φ(v) 的算法（F_n）

## 题面

判定∀φ 循环长 φ(u)=φ(v) 的算法（F_n）。

## 原文摘录

> 〔16.110 〕提出人：V. E. Shpilrain
> (I. Kapovich, P. Schupp). Is there an algorithm which, when given two elements u, v of a free group Fn, decides whether or not the cyclic length of ϕ(u) equals the cyclic length of ϕ(v) for every automorphism ϕ of Fn? Comment of 2009: The answer was shown to be positive for n = 2 in (D. Lee, J. Group Theory, 10 (2007), 561–569). V. E. Shpilrain
>
> —— Kourovka Notebook, Unsolved Problems in Group Theory, No. 21（E. I. Khukhro & V. D. Mazurov 编），问题 16.110（第16辑 2006 年）
