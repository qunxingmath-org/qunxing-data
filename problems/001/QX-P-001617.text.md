# 17.15 求保持多项式不动的最大自同构子群的算法

## 题面

构造算法：对给定 f∈Z[x₁,…,x_n]，显式给出 Aut(Z[x₁,…,x_n]) 中保持 f 不动的最大子群 G_f 的生成元。与丢番图方程 f=0 解集描述相关。

### 形式化表述

算法构造任务。

## 原文摘录

> 2010 辑问题 17.15，提出人：V. G. Bardakov〔batch_K二波〕
>
> Construct an algorithm which, for a given polynomial f ∈Z[x1, x2, . . . , xn],
> finds (explicitly, in terms of generators) the maximal subgroup Gf of the group
> Aut(Z[x1, x2, . . . , xn]) that leaves f fixed.
> This question is related to description
> of the solution set of the Diophantine equation f = 0.
> V. G. Bardakov
>
> —— Kourovka Notebook, Unsolved Problems in Group Theory, No. 21 (arXiv:1401.0300v50, 2026-11-02)
