# Is the Stone-Cech compactification of a widely-connected space necessarily an indecomposable continuum?

## 题面

widely-connected空间（任意两个非空开集皆连通）的Stone-Čech紧化是否必为不可分解连续统（indecomposable continuum）？

### 形式化表述

对每个widely-connected空间X，判断βX是否indecomposable；或构造反例（βX可分解的widely-connected X）。

## 原文摘录

> The following is an open problem in topology: Determine whether the Stone-Čech compactification of a widely-connected space is necessarily an indecomposable continuum.（D. Lipham, arXiv:1703.06862 摘要）
