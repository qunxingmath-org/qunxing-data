# 17.38 超可解形成" Radical"于饱和遗传形成类的刻画

## 题面

形成 F 在类 H 中 radical：F⊆H 且每个 H-群中任意两个正规 F-子群之积仍属 F。设 M 为使超可解形成在每成员中 radical 的饱和遗传形成类。刻画/判定 M（题面完整表述见原文）。

## 原文摘录

> 2010 辑问题 17.38，提出人：A. F. Vasil’ev, L. A. Shemetkov〔batch_K二波〕
>
> A formation F is called radical in a class H if F ⊆H and in every H-group
> the product of any two normal F-subgroups belongs to F. Let M be the class of all
> saturated hereditary formations of finite groups such that the formation of all finite
> supersoluble groups is radical in every element of M. Is it true that M has the largest
> (by inclusion) element?
> A. F. Vasil’ev, L. A. Shemetkov
>
> —— Kourovka Notebook, Unsolved Problems in Group Theory, No. 21 (arXiv:1401.0300v50, 2026-11-02)
