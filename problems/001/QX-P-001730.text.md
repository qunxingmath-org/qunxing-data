# 正态 CDF 在有理点取有理值

## 题面

标准正态 CDF 在有理点取有理值

## 原文摘录

> Suppose that we have rational numbers $q_1$, $q_2$ such that
> $$\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int_{-\infty}^{q_1}e^{-\frac{t^2}{2}} \,\mathrm{d}t=q_2.$$
> Does this imply that $q_1=0$ and $q_2=\dfrac{1}{2}$?
