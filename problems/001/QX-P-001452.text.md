# batch_I I-070：Hardy–Littlewood：h^p (0<p≤1) 中调和函数的共轭 v 满足 M_p(r,v)=

## 题面

Hardy–Littlewood：h^p (0<p≤1) 中调和函数的共轭 v 满足 M_p(r,v)=O((log 1/(1−r))^{1/p})；当 p=1,1/2,1/3,… 时 big-O 不能改进为 little-o。对其余 p∈(0,1] 是否也不能改进（即无 little-o 版）——至今是否仍开放？

### 形式化表述

∀p∈(0,1]\{1,1/2,1/3,…}: ∃h^p 调和函数其共轭 v 使 M_p(r,v) ≠ o((log 1/(1−r))^{1/p})？

## 原文摘录

> In Duren's book "Theory of $H^p$ spaces" ( MSN ) in the comment section after Section 4, it is mentioned that Littlewood and Hardy proved in Some properties of conjugate functions that if $u$ is a harmonic function in the unit disc belonging to the harmonic Hardy space $h^p$ , $0<p\leq 1$ .
