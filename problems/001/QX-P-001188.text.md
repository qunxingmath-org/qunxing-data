# Circuit-Hirsch 猜想：多胞体电路式直径 ≤ f−d

## 题面

d 维 f 面多胞体的电路式邻接直径 ≤ f−d。

### 形式化表述

∀d-多胞体 P（f 面）: 电路式邻接图直径 diam_circ(P) ≤ f−d。

## 原文摘录

> Circuit-Hirsch conjecture (cb-0073; arXiv:1611.08039): the diameter of the circuit-neighbourhood graph of a d-polytope with f facets is at most f−d.
